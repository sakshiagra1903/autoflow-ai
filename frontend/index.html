Detection methodology

01Feature engineering

Month-over-month changes, rolling 12-month baseline and numeric features.

02Rolling z-score

Flags observations at least 2 rolling standard deviations from baseline, when enough history exists.

03Isolation Forest

Unsupervised comparison model with a fixed random seed for reproducibility.

04Evidence verification

Unsupported real-world claims are withheld; demo records are never presented as official evidence.
